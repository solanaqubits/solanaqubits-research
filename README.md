# Solana Qubits Research

Markdown research materials from Solana Qubits.

This repository is intended for transparent, source-linked research notes about Solana validators, staking, ecosystem infrastructure, quantum technologies, post-quantum cryptography, and long-term technology literacy.

The stake pool research tracks public validator scoring dashboards, delegation dashboards, active application or interest links, and source-linked program updates.

The validator client landscape research distinguishes production clients, forks, hybrid implementations, schedulers, block-building systems, dashboards, and adjacent infrastructure using cautious, source-linked classifications.

The Jito Restaking research separates the restaking registry from the vault program, documents the mutual opt-in handshake between networks, vaults, and operators, and reports onchain account measurements alongside officially published totals rather than in place of them.

The quantum computing research snapshot tracks public scientific publications, standards documents, cloud/HPC context, vendor-stated roadmaps, and industry/adoption reports with cautious source attribution.

## Contents

- `articles/solana-stake-pool-delegation-landscape.md` — practical snapshot of Solana stake pool delegation programs for validators.
- `articles/solana-validator-client-landscape.md` — source-linked classification guide to Solana validator clients, forks, hybrid implementations, schedulers, block-building systems, dashboards, and adjacent infrastructure.
- `articles/jito-restaking-ncn-landscape.md` — structural and measurement guide to Jito Restaking: the restaking and vault programs, the NCN/vault/operator opt-in handshake and who initiates it, four access layers, named networks and operators, onchain account counts compared with published totals, operator fee data, slashing status, and a reproducible verification method.
- `articles/quantum-computing-in-2026.md` — cautious public research snapshot on quantum computing progress in 2026, including error correction, logical qubits, hardware approaches, benchmarks, cloud/HPC integration, adoption barriers, and post-quantum cryptography migration context.
- `sources/solana-stake-pool-links.md` — source links used for the stake pool delegation landscape research note, including public validator dashboards, delegation dashboards, application/interest links, and program updates.
- `sources/solana-validator-client-landscape-links.md` — primary repositories and documentation plus dashboard, methodology, and secondary sources used for the validator client landscape research note.
- `sources/jito-restaking-ncn-landscape-links.md` — protocol documentation, program repository, official interface pages, and reference implementations used for the Jito Restaking research note, plus the onchain measurement method and account layouts.
- `sources/quantum-computing-in-2026-links.md` — source links used for the quantum computing 2026 research snapshot, grouped by standards/PQC, peer-reviewed research, cloud/ecosystem, industry/adoption reports, and vendor/company materials.
